# ESA production is integrated over an hourly forecast, and nominal capacity stays a bench anchor

The ESA physics port reproduces `prototipo_water4all` faithfully. `referenceOutputKgPerDay = 0.3645 * 24 / 8.5` is the water one ACFF module collects per cycle at the 25 °C / 90 % RH bench measurement of the Li et al. prototype (`tests/test_esa_physics.py:27-30`, `src/h2o_farm/physics/esa.py:42-43`), and it is the divisor that converts a farm's nominal capacity into a module count. Mediterranean air is far drier than that anchor, so a farm realises roughly 41 % of nominal as an annual mean and under 10 % in July. That is the physics, not a calibration error.

The original implementation read a single instantaneous temperature and humidity sample and multiplied it across 24 hours. Read at a summer afternoon peak it returns zero, because the daily total is produced during the humid pre-dawn window. We therefore request an hourly temperature and humidity series from Open-Meteo — one extra parameter on the same free, key-less request — and integrate the engine across it as the prototype does, rather than extrapolating an instant. Offline, the synthetic generator must produce the same hourly shape, reconstructing humidity from a daily dew point and the diurnal temperature cycle (`src/h2o_farm/physics/weather.py:76-106`).

We keep nominal capacity anchored to the bench measurement instead of re-basing it on Mediterranean mean output, so the figure stays comparable with the prototype. The consequence is that a healthy plant reads far below 100 % of nominal, which is why the ratio is named **Ambient yield ratio** rather than an efficiency: it measures how dry the air is, not how well the equipment is working.
